# The_cook_App

**Tell the app what's in your kitchen and what you're aiming for, and it finds recipes that fit, with exact measurements.**

Cook App takes your main ingredients and your nutrition goals (for example, *pasta, low calories, high protein*), searches the internet for matching recipes, and gives you the full ingredient list with precise amounts.

---

## Features

- **Ingredient input**: add the main ingredients you already have (e.g. pasta, chicken, rice).
- **Nutrition goals**: set targets such as calorie limits and protein levels (e.g. low calories, high protein).
- **Internet recipe search**: automatically searches online for recipes that match your ingredients and goals.
- **Exact measurements**: every recipe includes precise quantities for each ingredient.
- **Nutrition info**: see calories and protein for each recipe so you can confirm it fits your goals.

---

##  How It Works

1. **Add your ingredients**: enter the main ingredients you have on hand.
2. **Set your requirements**: choose your calorie and protein goals.
3. **Search**: the app looks through the internet for recipes that match.
4. **Cook**: pick a recipe and follow the exact measurements.

### Example

| Input | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Main ingredient | Pasta |
| Calories | Low |
| Protein | High |

**Result:** a list of pasta recipes that are low in calories and high in protein, each with a full ingredient list and exact amounts.
